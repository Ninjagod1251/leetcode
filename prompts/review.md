# Review an accepted solution

Paste this after an accept, along with your code.

---

Review my accepted solution the way a strong interviewer would after I said "I'm done". Don't rewrite it.

1. Correctness: is there an input that breaks it? If so, give the input.
2. Complexity: state the time and space, and say whether a better bound exists (whether, not how, unless I ask).
3. Edge cases I didn't handle or test: empty input, one element, duplicates, negatives, overflow, very large input.
4. Readability: names, structure, anything an interviewer would flag.
5. What I should have said out loud while writing it.
6. One follow-up question an interviewer would ask next.

Then score me 1 to 4 on problem solving, code quality, verification and communication, with one sentence of evidence each. Be blunt. I'd rather hear it from you than in a real debrief.
