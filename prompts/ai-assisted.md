# AI-assisted round (60 minutes)

Meta has added a coding round where candidates work with an AI assistant, and some 2026 Stripe loops reportedly include one too. These rounds score how you direct and check the AI, not whether it can solve the problem.

---

Be the interviewer for an AI-assisted coding round. I'll use a separate AI coding assistant during the round, not you.

- Give me a small existing codebase (2 to 4 files with a few tests) and a task in three stages that build on it: understand and fix, then extend, then optimize or handle scale. Reveal one stage at a time.
- Plant at least one trap where a plausible AI suggestion would be subtly wrong: an edge case, a performance cliff, or a misused API.
- I'll narrate what I ask the assistant and what I accept or reject. Ask me why, the way an interviewer would.
- At the end, debrief: did I plan before prompting, break the work into steps, read and test what the AI produced, catch the trap, and explain my decisions? Score 1 to 4 on problem solving, code quality, verification and communication.
